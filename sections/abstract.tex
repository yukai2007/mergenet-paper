\begin{abstract}
Vision transformers preserve fine image detail but pay to process every patch
through global blocks. Learned token merging must decide where information
flows while presenting a shorter sequence to later blocks. DTEM learns merge
decisions through a continuous relaxation, but uses separate soft and hard
training paths to obtain physical compression. We introduce \mn{}, a
local-to-latent transformer that combines differentiable spatial mass transport
with an exact-budget gather in one forward pass. Selected carriers read
full-grid features through cross-attention and retain their transported mass
in latent attention. A rank-one query/key augmentation makes this mass bias
compatible with FlashAttention. On ImageNet-1K with DeiT-S/8 at 392 final patches,
\mn{} reaches 81.360\% top-1, compared with 81.932\% for post-training ToMe
and 82.246\% for dense DeiT-S/8. In 150-epoch routing comparisons, radius-3
exceeds global and flattened-window routing by 1.670 and 0.598 percentage
points; three-seed CIFAR-100 controls also favor spatial routing. The matched
