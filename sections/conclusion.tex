\section{Conclusion}

\mn{} combines differentiable spatial transport with an
exact-budget gather so that learned routing feeds a shorter latent
sequence. The radius-3 variant scores above the tested global and flattened
variants; a three-seed comparison also favors it over degree-matched routing.
The current ImageNet result is 81.360\% top-1 at 392 final patches,
with a clear accuracy gap to ToMe at that budget. Under a common
inference protocol, the trained-checkpoint accuracy--efficiency
tradeoff is \pendingresult{summary relative to baselines}. Reducing
full-grid and routing overhead remains a direction for further work.
