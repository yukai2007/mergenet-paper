\section{Conclusion}

\mn{} connects differentiable spatial mass transport to an exact-budget
latent sequence within one forward pass. Selected carriers retain their
transported mass and recover full-grid information before global reasoning;
an exact query/key augmentation supports the log-mass bias in FlashAttention.
On ImageNet-1K, the model reaches 81.360\% top-1 with 392 final patches
at 224 pixels and 82.618\% after 512-pixel fine-tuning.
Budget sweeps and implementation benchmarks characterize the interaction
between accuracy, resolution, and execution cost. The results motivate
further work on reducing full-grid overhead while retaining the trainable
physical bottleneck.
