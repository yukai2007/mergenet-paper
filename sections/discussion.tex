\section{Discussion}

The results separate the routing mechanism from overall model
accuracy. The exact gather shortens the latent sequence, and the original-grid
variant scores above the tested global and flattened-window variants on
ImageNet and three-seed CIFAR-100. At equal final patch counts, the complete
classifier trails ToMe on ImageNet. The routing-variant and cross-method
comparisons therefore answer different questions.

Deployment cost and component attribution require separate measurements
from the carrier count. Full-grid processing and routing overhead affect
runtime; the trained-checkpoint comparison gives
\pendingresult{latency and throughput interpretation}. ImageNet geometry
uses one seed, while the replicated locality test uses resized CIFAR-100.
The fixed-checkpoint component tests give
\pendingresult{mass-bias and recovery interpretation}. The routing
visualization describes transport without implying semantic boundary
preservation.
