\subsection{ImageNet geometry confirmation: reserved experiment}
\label{sec:pending-geometry}
\pendingslot{P04}{Replicated ImageNet support controls}{These cells are
intentionally blank and are not included in the reported results.}

The proposed extension compares global, flattened-window, radius-3 and
degree-matched support with identical depth, learning rate, stochastic depth,
selector and final budget. The degree control preserves realized per-donor
counts after the same donor/receiver partition while changing spatial
correspondence. Seeds and the final-epoch endpoint must be fixed before
launch. Existing single-seed results may be reused only after recipe identity
is verified. CIFAR standard deviations cannot substitute for ImageNet uncertainty.
