\subsection{Measured routing structure: reserved visualization}
\label{sec:pending-routing}
\pendingslot{P06}{Trace actual routing on trained ImageNet examples}{The
figure is reserved for measured outputs, not an architectural illustration.}

The final ImageNet checkpoint is unavailable locally, so the collaborator
exports four to six validation examples, including at least two correct and
two incorrect predictions. For every routing step the record contains the
actual donor/receiver partition, nonzero transport edges, token-mass map and
final carriers; one or two carrier recovery-attention maps are added when
available. The raw trace accompanies the rendered figure.

The trace must be passive: we record the maximum logit difference with tracing
enabled, equality of top-$K$ indices, and the mass-conservation error at every
step. The default flattened-index center cannot reconstruct complete source
membership. Without a verified membership trace, the visualization therefore
shows per-step transport rather than inventing a final hard segmentation.
Local edges alone do not establish semantic-boundary preservation; that claim
would require an annotation source and an explicit metric.
