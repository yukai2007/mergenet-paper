\paragraph{Classifier.}
The patch-8 model has width 384, six heads, six local blocks, and six latent
blocks. At 224 pixels, the local encoder processes 784 patch slots. Local
attention spans 16 sequence positions on either side of a patch query;
the CLS query attends globally. Spatial routing uses radius 3, a
$384\to64$ metric projection, similarity temperature 1, and metric
input-gradient scale 0.1. At compression factor 2, the six soft donor
budgets are $(65,65,65,65,65,67)$; the hard gather retains 392 patches.
The latent encoder does not merge further. Stochastic depth is 0.1,
with separate local and latent ramps.

\paragraph{Routing and selection.}
Training partitions donors and receivers randomly for each image;
evaluation alternates deterministic odd/even partitions across routing
steps. The threshold-based soft top-$k$ uses scale 30 and normalizes
energies by their max-minus-min range. The final gather indices are
detached, and selected carriers are ordered by a detached,
mass-weighted center of their original flattened indices. Spatial
scoring uses sparse neighbor lists; the global and flattened-window
controls use their respective historical dense-scoring paths.
At training time, an auxiliary full-grid readout contributes up to
0.05 to the latent CLS logits. Its weight begins ramping at epoch 10
for the 150-epoch runs and epoch 20 for the 300-epoch run, reaching
0.05 over 10 and 20 epochs, respectively. It is disabled at evaluation.

\paragraph{CIFAR-100 controls.}
\label{app:cifar-protocol}
The three-seed routing comparison uses 12 independent 200-epoch runs:
global, flattened-window, radius-3, and degree-matched routing for
seeds 42--44. Native $32\times32$ images are resized to 224 pixels.
The primary endpoint is epoch-199 EMA top-1 on the 10,000-image test
set. Each training and evaluation batch contains 200 images. The
degree-matched arm permutes receiver columns of the realized radius-3
support using fixed receiver priorities; it preserves each donor's
candidate count and the receiver-degree multiset. Table~\ref{tab:cifar-seeds}
reports means and sample standard deviations across seeds, with paired
same-seed differences.
