\subsection{Efficiency}
\label{sec:systems}

At 224 pixels, \mn{} passes 392 rather than 784 patches to the
latent stage and requires an estimated 14.13 GMAC per image, versus
22.41 GMAC for dense DeiT-S/8. The estimate excludes indexing and
data movement.

Table~\ref{tab:a100-efficiency} and Figure~\ref{fig:a100-efficiency}
summarize the random-weight A100 measurements. Appendix~\ref{app:followup}
