\section{Introduction}

Fine patch grids preserve object boundaries and small visual details, while
global self-attention integrates evidence across an image. Their cost grows
with the token sequence. Token merging can consolidate redundant patches
instead of discarding them~\citep{tome2023}, but a learned merger must solve
two coupled problems: decide where information should flow, and expose later
layers to the physically shorter sequence that realizes the savings.
% 

Continuous relaxations make routing trainable. DTEM learns merge embeddings
through soft grouping and merging~\citep{dtem2024}, but its continuous path
retains all token slots; its end-to-end protocol alternates relaxed merge
updates with hard token-reducing backbone updates. We ask whether one forward
path can learn continuous routing while making the downstream model train on
the exact token budget used at inference.

\mn{} answers this question with a local-to-latent architecture. A full-grid
encoder performs soft mass transport, then an exact-budget gather changes the
shape from $N$ patches to $K$ carriers. Residual cross-attention recovers
evidence from the full-grid local features, and global reasoning occurs only
on the $K$ carriers. Routing is restricted to receiver positions within
radius $R$ on the original two-dimensional grid. This is a weak spatial
prior: it excludes implausible long jumps but leaves edge weights, mass, and
carrier locations learned.

Two details connect the formulation to efficient execution. We use the
threshold-adjusted smooth top-$k$ construction of \citet{sujianlin2024topk}
and couple it to hard indices with a straight-through carrier weight. The
gather indices are discrete; the claim is gradient flow to their continuous
scores, not differentiation of the indices themselves. We also preserve a
carrier's accumulated mass as a $\log m$ key bias. Because this bias is rank
one across queries, an augmented query/key dimension realizes it through an
unmodified FlashAttention call~\citep{flashattn2022}.

Our evidence supports the routing mechanism while keeping the deployment
claim bounded. Spatial routing outperforms global and flattened controls on
ImageNet and across three CIFAR-100 seeds; a degree-matched
receiver permutation shows that locality contributes beyond candidate
cardinality. The trained 300-epoch model, however, trails dense DeiT-S/8 and
post-training ToMe in the available matched-budget accuracy records. We
therefore do not claim a state-of-the-art frontier before common-protocol
checkpoint timing and the matched DTEM row are available.

Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a continuous spatial-routing path followed by an exact physical
  bottleneck, so the latent encoder trains and executes on a fixed carrier
  budget;
  \item an original-grid routing prior whose gain is tested against global,
  flattened-window and degree-matched controls; and
  \item mass-aware attention with an exact FlashAttention-compatible
  reparameterization, together with an evaluation that separates accuracy,
  token count, arithmetic cost, and measured latency.
\end{itemize}
