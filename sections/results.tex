\subsection{Main Results}
\label{sec:results}
\label{sec:primary-results}

At the 392-patch operating point, \mn{} reaches 81.360\% ImageNet
top-1 accuracy (Table~\ref{tab:main}). ToMe and PiToMe, applied to the
dense checkpoint after training, reach 81.932\% and 81.742\%;
uncompressed DeiT-S/8 reaches 82.246\%. Thus the proposed model
learns its routing policy while processing a physically shorter latent
sequence, although the available ToMe configuration retains more
classification accuracy at the same final patch count. The matched
DTEM-p8 adaptation reaches \pendingvalue\% top-1.

The budget sweep shows how these models behave away from the default
operating point (Figure~\ref{fig:sweep}). Using the same \mn{}
checkpoint, increasing the final budget from 196 to 523 patches
raises top-1 from 80.228\% to 81.380\%. ToMe remains ahead at each
of the five 224-pixel budgets and at the five 384-pixel
fine-tuned budgets shown in the figure. The complete sweep contains
20 matched final-budget settings, all with the same ordering
(Appendix~\ref{app:latency}). These are evaluations of fixed
checkpoints; they describe accuracy as the inference budget changes
rather than the outcome of retraining at each budget.

\subsection{Ablation Studies}
\label{sec:cifar-seeds}

Among the tested \mn{} routing variants, the original-grid radius-3
configuration scores above the global and flattened-window configurations.
With the same 150-epoch ImageNet recipe and learning rate, radius 3 reaches
78.888\% top-1, compared with 77.218\% for global routing and
78.290\% for a flattened width-8 window
(Table~\ref{tab:geometry}). Radius 2 is slightly higher at
78.942\%; these single-seed results identify a useful routing configuration,
not an optimal radius. The global arm retains \mn{}'s architecture
and is an internal variant rather than a DTEM reproduction. The variants
also use their respective scoring implementations, detailed in
Appendix~\ref{app:recipe}.

Radius also changes the number of eligible receivers. We therefore
add a CIFAR-100 control that permutes radius-3 receivers while
preserving donor candidate counts and receiver degrees
(Appendix~\ref{app:cifar-protocol}).

Across three seeds on resized CIFAR-100, radius 3 has the highest
epoch-199 EMA top-1 mean among the four routing policies
(Table~\ref{tab:cifar-seeds}). Its paired gain over the
degree-matched control is $1.42\pm0.30$ points, with all three
seed-wise differences positive. This supports the spatial prior
within the tested setting; the replicated result does not estimate
ImageNet seed variance.
